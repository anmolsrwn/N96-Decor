About N96 Decor

We infuse meaning into weddings

N96 Decor is a wedding decor, florals and set design studio founded by Anubhuti Jain and Naghmi Hamrapurkar. We believe your venue should turn into your own little world, one that tells your story and reflects your personality, taste and style.

A custom-made cushion, your favourite poem on the dinner table, or your dog on every coaster: we take decor beyond flowers, fabrics and pillars, and create spaces that leave guests in awe and make them feel valued.
